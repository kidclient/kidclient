# 📋 KidClient — Game Spec & Client Brief Template

Copy and fill out this short brief with your child during your 5-minute discovery chat, then paste it right below the **[`KID_GAME_SYSTEM_PROMPT.md`](../prompts/KID_GAME_SYSTEM_PROMPT.md)** into your favorite AI!

---

```markdown
# CLIENT BRIEF FOR AI DEVELOPER

- **Client Name & Age:** [e.g., Leo, Age 4]
- **Game Title Idea:** [e.g., Shark Truck Ice Cream Party]
- **Game Style / Track:** [Choose one: 3D World (Three.js) / 2D Arcade (Canvas) / Voice & Story Toy]

## 1. The Hero (Who am I playing as?)
- **Appearance:** [e.g., A bright blue monster truck with a shark fin on top and giant bouncy tires]
- **How it moves:** [e.g., Drives around a sunny green island and can jump super high]

## 2. The Goal (What do we collect or do?)
- **Things to collect/do:** [e.g., Drive into giant floating ice cream cones to chomp them]
- **What happens when we get one:** [e.g., Every ice cream cone makes the truck grow 5% bigger and plays a happy chime! At 10 cones, confetti rains from the sky]

## 3. The World & Obstacles
- **Environment:** [e.g., Green grassy island with ramps, rainbow bridges, and bouncy trees]
- **Silly Obstacles (No Game Over!):** [e.g., Puddles of mud that make the truck spin in a funny circle and say "WHOA!"]

## 4. The "Big Silly Button" (Action Button B)
- **What happens when the player taps the Silly Button:** [e.g., Shoots a giant pancake out of the front that knocks over towers of blocks + plays a silly horn sound]

## 5. Special Client Requests
- [e.g., "Make sure there is a friendly yellow dog following my truck everywhere!"]
```
