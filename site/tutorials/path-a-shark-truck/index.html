1 The 5-Minute 4yo Toy Pitch

Ask your child these 4 questions while sketching on paper before touching any AI tool:

# CLIENT BRIEF FOR AI DEVELOPER
- Client: Age 4 Creative Director
- Game Style: Matte Clay & Painted Wood Toybox 3D World (Three.js Single-File HTML5)
- The Hero: A cobalt-blue clay monster truck with a shark fin, googly eyes, yellow hubcaps, and big matte rubber tires.
- The Goal: Drive around a warm wooden toy island and chomp floating clay ice cream cones! Every cone plays a chime and grows the truck bigger.
- No-Losing Rule: Surround the island with a coral bouncy bumper ring so the truck never falls off—hitting the edge bounces you back with a "BOING!"
- Knock-Over Toys: Add painted wooden block towers and yellow/coral stunt ramps!
- The Big Silly Button: A chunky coral button inside a yellow toybox controller that launches 3 buttered pancakes out of the front of the truck!

2 Paste the System Prompt Into Any Free AI

Copy this right above your child’s brief in free Claude Artifacts, ChatGPT Canvas, Gemini, or Antigravity:

You are the Lead Game Developer for "KidClient Toybox Studio." Take the 4-year-old Client Brief below and output a complete, runnable, tablet-ready 3D game in a SINGLE self-contained index.html file using Three.js (https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js).

Rules:
1. TACTILE TOYBOX AESTHETIC: Warm studio daylight, matte MeshStandardMaterial (roughness 0.86) polymer-clay & painted wooden toy colors, zero broken external files.
2. WEB AUDIO + VOICE SYNTH: Build a native AudioContext synthesizer for boings, chimes, and silly horn sounds, plus window.speechSynthesis cheers every 5 points.
3. IPAD TOUCH CONTROLS: Include touch-action:none CSS, a chunky bottom-left yellow arcade thumbstick box, and bottom-right matte coral "Silly!" and green "Jump!" buttons.
4. TODDLER UX: Zero Game Over screens! Forgiving collection hitboxes + localStorage high-score saving.

3 Turn AI Mistakes Into Teaching Moments

🛞 “Why are the wheels spinning sideways?”

AI doesn’t have eyes—it guessed the wrong axis! Let your child direct the fix: “Roll the tires on the X axis.”

🥞 “Make the Silly Button even sillier!”

Let your 4yo be QA Tester: change numbers together (3 pancakes → 10 pancakes) to see cause and effect live.
