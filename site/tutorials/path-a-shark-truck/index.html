Step 1: Run the 5-Minute "Client Discovery Call"

Sit down with your child and ask them the 4 KidClient questions. Here is the exact brief our 4-year-old dictated for Shark-Truck Island:

# CLIENT BRIEF FOR AI DEVELOPER
- Client: Tiny Boss (Age 4)
- Game Style: 3D Driving & Stunt World (Three.js Single-File HTML5)
- The Hero: A bright blue monster truck with a shark fin on the roof, yellow KidClient glasses, orange wheel rims, and big monster tires.
- The Goal: Drive around a sunny green island and chomp giant floating ice cream cones! Every cone plays a happy chime and makes the truck grow bigger.
- Kid-Safe Rules: Surround the island with a glowing red bouncy bumper ring so the truck never falls off—hitting the edge bounces you back with a "BOING!"
- Knock-Over Toys: Add colorful 3D block towers and purple jump ramps!
- The Big Silly Button: A giant red "SILLY BUTTON" that blasts 3 buttered pancakes out of the front of the truck!

Step 2: Paste the KidClient System Prompt Into Any Free AI

Copy the prompt below and paste it into Claude (Free Artifacts), ChatGPT (Canvas), Google AI Studio (Gemini), or Antigravity right above your child's brief:

You are the Lead Game Developer for "KidClient Studios." Take the 4-year-old Client Brief below and output a complete, runnable, tablet-ready 3D game in a SINGLE self-contained index.html file using Three.js (https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js).

Rules:
1. ZERO EXTERNAL BROKEN ASSETS: Build the Shark-Truck, ice cream cones, ramps, and block towers using Three.js 3D primitives (BoxGeometry, CylinderGeometry, ConeGeometry, SphereGeometry, TorusGeometry).
2. SOUND & VOICE SYNTH: Include a Web Audio API (AudioContext) sound synthesizer for boings, coin chimes, and silly horn sounds, plus window.speechSynthesis spoken praise every 5 points!
3. TABLET & IPAD CONTROLS: Include touch-action:none CSS, a bottom-left virtual thumbstick, and two giant bottom-right circular buttons: a green JUMP button and a glowing red "SILLY BUTTON" that shoots 3D pancakes.
4. TODDLER UX: No game-over or death screens! Use bouncy ring bumpers, forgiving collection hitboxes, and save the high score to localStorage.

Step 3: Turn AI Quirks Into Teaching Moments

🛞 "Why are the wheels spinning sideways?"

Show your child that AI doesn't have eyes—it guessed the wrong rotation axis! Tell the AI together: "Rotate the wheels on the X axis instead of Z."

🥞 "Make the Silly Button 10x crazier!"

Let your child test QA and ask to change numbers: "What happens if we shoot 3 pancakes at once and make them bounce off the floor?"
