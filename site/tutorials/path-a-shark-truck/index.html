STEP 01 The 5-Minute Client Brief

Fill out these 4 questions with your child before touching any AI tool:

# CLIENT BRIEF FOR AI DEVELOPER
- Client: Age 4 Creative Director
- Game Style: Matte Clay / Toybox 3D World (Three.js Single-File HTML5)
- The Hero: A Klein-blue monster truck with a shark fin, matte clay googly eyes with yellow glasses, and big rubber tires.
- The Goal: Drive around a warm wooden toy island and chomp floating ice cream cones! Every cone plays a chime and grows the truck bigger.
- Kid-Safe Rule (No Losing!): Surround the island with a coral bouncy bumper ring so the truck never falls off—hitting the edge bounces you back with a "BOING!"
- Knock-Over Toys: Add painted wooden block towers and crayon-colored stunt ramps!
- The Big Red Silly Button: A chunky tactile red button that launches 3 buttered pancakes out of the front of the truck!

STEP 02 Drop-In System Prompt (Works With Any Free AI)

Paste this right above your child’s brief in free Claude Artifacts, ChatGPT Canvas, Gemini, or Antigravity:

You are the Lead Game Developer for "KidClient." Take the 4-year-old Client Brief below and output a complete, runnable, tablet-ready 3D game in a SINGLE self-contained index.html file using Three.js (https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js).

Rules:
1. TACTILE TOYBOX AESTHETIC: Use warm studio daylight, matte MeshStandardMaterial (roughness 0.85) clay & painted wood tones, and zero broken external files.
2. WEB AUDIO + VOICE SYNTH: Build a native AudioContext synthesizer for boings, coin chimes, and silly horns, plus window.speechSynthesis cheers every 5 points.
3. IPAD TOUCH CONTROLS: Include touch-action:none CSS, a chunky bottom-left yellow toybox thumbstick pad, and two bottom-right physical arcade buttons (Green JUMP + Red SILLY BTN).
4. TODDLER UX: Zero Game Over screens! Forgiving collection hitboxes + localStorage high-score persistence.

STEP 03 Teach AI Literacy Through Bugs

🛞 “Why are the wheels spinning sideways?”

AI doesn’t have eyes—it guessed the wrong axis! Let your child direct the fix: “Roll the tires on the X axis.”

🥞 “Make the Silly Button crazier!”

Let your 4yo be QA Tester: change numbers together (3 pancakes → 10 pancakes) to teach cause and effect.
