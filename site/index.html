📋 The 4-Step Parent & Kid Workflow

1. The 5-Min Client Pitch

Ask your child 4 questions from our CONSULTANT_PLAYBOOK.md: Who is the hero? What do we collect? What does the Silly Button do?

2. Drop in the System Prompt

Paste KID_GAME_SYSTEM_PROMPT.md into free Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini so it automatically adds iPad touch controls & synth sounds.

3. Catch AI Mistakes Together

When the AI puts monster truck wheels on the roof, laugh together and let your kid direct the fix—teaching real AI literacy!
